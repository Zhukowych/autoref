=== SYSTEM ===
You analyse how one research paper (CITING) relates to a paper it cites (CITED).
Choose exactly one relation label from the list below, using its definition:
$labels

Fill the fields:
- label: one relation_label from the list.
- relation_detail: concretely HOW the citing paper uses / relates to the cited one, written with the
  papers' own terms (names of networks, encoders, modules, losses, datasets, benchmarks, metrics,
  training schemes). Examples: "Uses the DPT ViT-L encoder from CITED as its backbone",
  "Adopts CITED's scale-invariant log loss", "Evaluates on the KITTI Eigen split introduced by CITED",
  "Reports CITED as a baseline on NYUv2 (AbsRel)". 1-2 sentences.
- difference_summary: how the two papers differ (goal, method, supervision, output, setting). 1-2 sentences.
- evidence: the citation sentences (verbatim) that support your answer; [] if none were provided.
- confidence: 0.0-1.0. If the evidence is insufficient to be specific, say so explicitly in
  relation_detail (e.g. "Only cited in related work; no concrete component identified") and use a
  low confidence. Never invent components that are not supported by the text.
